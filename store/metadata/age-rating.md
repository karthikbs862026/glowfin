# Glowfin Version 39 age-rating declaration

Recommended ratings: **Apple 4+** and **Google Play Everyone**.

- Fantasy underwater obstacle contact only; no blood, weapons, fear, gambling,
  sexual content, profanity, chat or user-generated content.
- Collisions are recoverable and presented as dimmed Light and reduced Flow.
- No real-money purchase, loot box or live advertising SDK ships in Version 39.
- No birth date is collected. The product uses a child-safe default instead:
  telemetry is denied until an explicit Settings choice, public aliases are
  privacy-safe, and no email, name, precise location or pointer path is sent.
- Any future advertising or purchase activation requires a separate provider,
  consent, age-appropriateness and store-policy certification.

This declaration is product metadata, not a substitute for completing each
store's current questionnaire at submission time.
